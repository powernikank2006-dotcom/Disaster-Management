Emergency Preparedness

Keep essential items ready before a disaster occurs.

First Aid Kit

Keep medicines, bandages and essential medical supplies ready.

Water

Store sufficient clean drinking water for emergencies.

Power Bank

Keep your mobile phone and emergency communication devices charged.

Emergency Radio

Keep a battery-powered radio for emergency announcements.

Flashlight

Keep a working flashlight and spare batteries.

Important Documents

Keep copies of important identification and emergency documents.

Emergency Response

During an emergency, stay calm and follow instructions from local authorities.
